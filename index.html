Research & Projects

Information Gathering for Manipulation Under Occlusion

Ongoing MS thesis research · University of Utah

My thesis investigates how a robot can decide when it has enough information to attempt a planned motion in a cluttered scene. The robot maintains a probabilistic belief about occupancy and evaluates the planned motion against that belief. When more information is needed, it chooses between lifting an object to reveal hidden space and moving an object out of the way.

The method currently runs in kinematic simulation on a 20-scene benchmark. Real-robot validation is the next step.

Real-Robot Pick-and-Place System

Robotics systems development · LL4MA Lab

I build and maintain a pick-and-place system on a KUKA arm. This work involves integrating perception, grasping, motion planning, calibration, and execution recovery into a working robot pipeline.
